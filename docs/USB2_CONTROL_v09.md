# USB2 controls for v0.9

Selected control circuit; use the current v0.9 full-board checks and quote status.

The board's USB-C cable carries audio output, two Dynamixel serial buses, three PWM controls through the existing CH334F four-port USB2 hub. The OAK-D camera and microphone array connect directly to the Jetson or a purchased USB3 hub. They do not use this board's USB power or hub. The audio, serial and PWM USB devices share this hub and its local supply; a failure there can disconnect all three functions.

The former camera 5 V converter becomes the local USB controls supply. Its existing TPS54560 power stage and feedback remain; nominal output is 4.96 V. This removes the custom USB3 hub, SuperSpeed muxes, USB3 connectors, 8 A camera/microphone supply and their fine connector escape requirements. The retained converter's 5 A IC rating is not a measured board output rating; this controls-only load is much smaller.

## Power and host attachment

The local supply first passes an always-enabled TPS2553 current limiter, then the revised FB1 filter bead and `V5_USB_HUB`. The upstream limiter is set by 20 kΩ: approximately 1.183–1.397 A including its external1% resistor and±100ppm/°C resistor TCR over−40…85°C. FB1 becomes Murata BLM18PG121SN1D, still 0603, rated 2 A at 85 °C with 0.05 Ω maximum DC resistance. This protects the hub-side rail without overloading the former 1 A bead. The filter impedance changes from 600 Ω to 120 Ω at 100 MHz; check noise and EMI during bring-up. The 2 A figure does not extend to 125 °C, where Murata specifies 1 A. The hub/switch front-end parts have an85°C ambient ceiling, but the fitted CM108B codec specifies **0–70°C operating ambient**; this is not an85°C-rated whole board. [C-Media CM108B v1.41 §8.2](https://www.cmedia.com.tw/storage/upload/sync_file/E02-0045%20CM108B_Datasheet_v1.41.pdf) [Murata manufacturer datasheet, Arrow mirror](https://static6.arrow.com/aropdfconversion/ba86d7abd322459e2147c87f542df6d24c7e39d5/blm18pg121sn1.pdf) That rail powers CH334F pin19 and C60/C61/C62. The hub's 3.3 V LDO output remains `H_V33`, with C63/C64 unchanged. A separate active-low TPS2552 power switch supplies the existing `V5_USB` rail used by the audio, dual UART and one PWM control MCU. CH334F pin24 `PWREN#` controls the switch, and its independent `FAULT#` feeds hub pin1 `OVCUR#`. The separate child limit is below the upstream limit even after the documented current allocations, so an ordinary child overload can report through the powered hub. A hub-side supply fault intentionally trips the upstream hardware protection and may reset the hub. Verify both cases on the bench. The 37.5 kΩ current-limit setting screens to approximately 624–774 mA including resistor tolerance/TCR, process and temperature; the expected aggregate child load must be measured below its minimum limit. This is a fixed embedded-device power budget, not general-purpose 500 mA sockets. [TI TPS2552](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2552.pdf)

CH334F pin18 `PSELF` is open: its internal pull-up selects self-powered operation. Pin16 `RESET#/CDP` floats normally; driving it strongly high at startup selects a different operating mode. The existing internal reset is retained. Hub port3 pins7/8 are unconnected. Pins13/21 are unused; the optional configuration EEPROM and its wiring are omitted. No USB5744 control signal or RC fault-hold circuit remains. The implementation follows the manufacturer's separate ganged power-control and overcurrent inputs. [WCH CH334 v2.5](https://cdn-learn.adafruit.com/assets/assets/000/131/435/original/CH334DS1.PDF?1721660148=)

J10 host `VBUS_IN` supplies only the ESD clamp reference, 100 nF bypass, 10 kΩ discharge resistor and a high-impedance sense divider. USB D+/D− pass through the `H_V33`-powered TS3USB221A switch. A mandatory **TLV3011BIDBVR** comparator enables the data path only while host VBUS exceeds approximately 3.97 V. Its 220 kΩ/100 kΩ divider feeds IN−; the internal reference feeds IN+. Its open-drain output pulls switch OE# low for valid VBUS, while the existing 100 kΩ pull-up disables the switch otherwise. The B suffix is required for fail-safe inputs and the specified high-impedance power-on-reset output; the original TLV3011 is not an approved substitute. The earlier Schmitt-gate proposal was rejected because the USBLC6 steering diode could feed an attached local D+ pull-up back into raw VBUS and hold a low-threshold sense gate on. A minimum trip above the hub's 3.5 V maximum LDO output removes that feedback condition. [TI TS3USB221A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ts3usb221a.pdf), [TI TLV3011B](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv3011.pdf)

The comparator uses a local 100 nF supply bypass and a 1 nF reference bypass, below TI's 10 nF stable reference-load limit. The documented full-temperature reference/offset/hysteresis/resistor screen gives a 3.717–4.243 V trip envelope; an additional CMRR/PSRR engineering allowance expands this to 3.692–4.269 V. That extra allowance is not a new manufacturer guarantee. The 100 nF/10 kΩ raw-VBUS discharge screen reaches the lower threshold from 5.5 V in approximately 0.515 ms at the stated maximum RC corner; verify actual unplug and host-off timing, power sequencing, slow ramps and backfeed on hardware. **U9903 requires manual fitting:** JLC had no stock of the exact B-version part at review time. The fabrication/assembly order must leave its footprint unpopulated and the walkthrough must require fitting it before USB use.

The 3.3 V external load allocation is conservatively 8 mA, including the existing indicator and control pull-ups with spare allocation, below the hub LDO's published 20 mA external-load capability. The selected USB switch is TS3USB221ARSER in the RSE UQFN10 package, with 0.50 mm pitch and no exposed pad. The stock KiCad Texas UQFN10 footprint matches TI drawing 4220307/A. Its adjacent USB pins permit short straight paired routes without signal vias. The earlier TS3USB30E VSSOP alternative is not populated.

## Disable behavior

The local controls converter's `O_EN` may be pulled to ground through the shared section-disable header. This disables hub, audio USB, Dynamixel USB and PWM USB together. Host VBUS must never be shorted by that header. Converter disable is a low-power service control; the external mechanical disconnect remains the battery isolation switch. An independently powered servo or motor interface can remain electrically active after USB power is disabled, so verify default-disabled PWM, Dynamixel buffer power-off behavior with their loads connected.

## Integration changes

Use `usb2_control.apply_base_parts()` on a cloned v0.6 inventory before integration net mapping, then append its 33 support entries: 22 components, including one mandatory manual-fit comparator, plus 11 bare integration landings. These landings are PCB test/link pads rather than fitted connectors. Remove U18/C68 and all old host-VBUS-to-FB1 copper. Change C67 to the selected **100 nF** VBUS sense bypass. Rename the old converter output `+5V_OAK` to `USB_CONTROL_5V`. Re-route U10 upstream pins14/15 through the new switch; preserving the old direct USB traces would bypass host disconnect. Move the hub's own supply and three input capacitors to `V5_USB_HUB`; retain child devices on the downstream switch output `V5_USB`. FB1 input is `USB2_LIMITED_5V`, the upstream switch output; its output remains `V5_USB_HUB`. C60/C62 are ordinary ceramic capacitors with pin1 positive rail/pin2 ground; C61 likewise. C9902 is polarized: pin1 `V5_USB`, pin2 ground.

New no-connect list for U10 is 2,7,8,13,16,17,18,21,23; remove only pin24 from inherited NC declarations. The EEPROM is omitted to keep the proven audio/serial routing and simplify the tightly packed hub escape. PSELF alone selects self-powered operation. Capture self-powered descriptors, removable-port flags and declared device currents at cold boot. Default removable flags do not prevent the three fixed functions from operating. Do not invent VID/PID/string bytes. Confirm startup, enumeration, all functions together, suspend/resume and fault recovery before operating the robot.

## Release and bench holds

Before fabrication: finish actual integrated placement/routing and native DRC; independently audit source-to-pad net consistency, host/local power separation, USB pair/reference geometry, all newly changed footprints and manufacturer drill rules. The HRO TYPE-C-31-M-12 manufacturer drawing has been inspected; its pin pattern and slot geometry are retained. The main-board2oz variant enlarges shell lands to1.12×1.72mm and1.12×2.22mm without moving or enlarging the slots. The nominal annulus/drill screen checks those actual pads. Confirm the factory's finished stackup thickness and delivered connector seating/tail engagement; drawing inspection alone does not establish assembled mechanical fit.

Bench: measure local 5 V ripple/startup and hub/child voltage at maximum simultaneous load, USB supply current and 3.3 V LDO temperature; verify host-off/board-off leakage and all section-disable states; short the child rail with a controlled supply and observe current limit, overcurrent reporting and recovery; verify all USB devices enumerate and operate together. The 4.96 V feedback divider corners from initial 1% resistor tolerance are 4.829–5.094 V before bead/switch/wiring losses, resistor drift and ripple, so verify the loaded endpoint remains within each device's operating range. [TI TPS54560](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps54560.pdf)

Exact live supplier models and stock snapshots are in `outputs/usb2-control/jlc-*.json`; electrical screening is in `circuit-screen.json`. They are sourcing evidence, not an assembled PCB quote or fabrication release.

## Routed support cell checks

The separate 45×30 mm work product provides the support-circuit copper for integration into the main PCB. Host landing J9900 has D+ at (28,5.25) and D− at (28,5.75) mm; hub landing J9901 has D+ at (2,4.75) and D− at (2,5.25) mm. Both data segments use front-layer copper over continuous inner-layer ground, without signal vias. The nominal 90 Ω target uses 0.165 mm width and 0.300 mm gap for the selected JLC041621-2116 1.6 mm / 4-layer / 2 oz outer stack; obtain production-file confirmation. Integrating these pads must not create an open-ended same-net branch. The cell check does not qualify the complete USB connector-to-hub channel.

The prototype omits the optional descriptor EEPROM and its wiring. CH334F PSELF remains open/high to select self-powered operation; pins 13 and 21 are unused. Default removable-port descriptors permit the three embedded functions to operate. Before deployment, capture the hub descriptors at cold boot, confirm self-powered status and ganged power/overcurrent behavior, and check all three child devices enumerate and suspend correctly. A validated complete EEPROM image is outside this prototype.

The supply-current references and calculations from v0.8 remain component evidence; their old four-function CAN allocation is conservative historical context, not a fourth populated port. Current v0.9 has audio, UART and PWM only. The new board/fresh evidence identifies the selected three-channel architecture explicitly.
