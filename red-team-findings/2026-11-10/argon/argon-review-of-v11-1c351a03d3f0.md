#### 6. Audit Script (`audit_v11.py`) Blind Spots
`audit_v11.py` accurately verifies that the 291 non-servo footprints, pads, tracks, and zones above `y = 110.5` match v0.10 and that schematic-to-board netlists match. However, it has **five significant blind spots**:
1. **Does not audit `CPL_JLCPCB.csv` rotations against package pin-1 / cathode geometry:** `export_v11.py` only asserts that unmoved parts kept their v0.10 CPL rotation and moved parts changed by `turn`. Because v0.10's CPL rotations were never audited part-by-part, `export_v11.py` faithfully propagated the 90°-rotated `SOT-23`s and 180°-reversed `D_SMC` diodes into v0.11.
2. **Does not check via count or trace width on high-current / surge pads inside the servo block:** It cannot catch that `D7001` (`SMCJ36CA`) and `C7111` (`GND`) are necked down to a single `0.22 mm` trace and a single `0.30 mm` via.
3. **Does not check cross-layer plane overlap hazards:** It does not flag that `PWM_VIN` (33.6 V) on `B.Cu` and `PWM_5V` (5 V) on `In2.Cu` overlap across `1,242 mm²` or that `F7000` Pad 2 (`PWM_VIN`) punches through `PWM_5V` with `0.30 mm` clearance.
4. **Does not check `F.Cu` zone island removal around SMD pads:** It did not catch that `ISLAND_REMOVAL_MODE_ALWAYS` deleted the `F.Cu` `GND` pour around `C7111` Pad 2 and `D7001` Pad 2 because their own silkscreen/courtyard/pad geometries pinched off the `0.25 mm` minimum pour width.
5. **Allows dangling vias/tracks if total DRC warning count ≤ v0.10 (`sum(kinds.values()) <= sum(base.values())`):** Because one `track_dangling` warning disappeared in v0.11 (`10 → 9`), `audit_v11.py` allowed a new `via_dangling` warning (`1 → 2`) to pass silently.

#### 9. Independent Recalculation of Thor 24 V Buck (`U2` LM5146RGYR) Current Limit with `R13 = 220 Ω`
In `LM5146RGYR` (`U2`), low-side $R_{\text{DS(on)}}$ current sensing sets the valley inductor current threshold:
$$I_{\text{valley}} = \frac{I_{\text{RDSON}} \cdot R_{13} + V_{\text{ACL(offset)}}}{R_{\text{DS(on),Q3}}(T_J)}$$
* **Parameters from TI `LM5146` and Infineon `BSC034N06NS` (`Q3`) datasheets:**
  * $R_{13} = 220\ \Omega \pm 1\%$ (`217.8` to `222.2 Ω`).
  * $I_{\text{RDSON}}$ (`LM5146` current source at $25^\circ\text{C}$): `180 µA` min / `200 µA` typ / `220 µA` max (with a positive TC of $\approx +0.3\%/^\circ\text{C}$ designed to track MOSFET $R_{\text{DS(on)}}$ over temperature).
  * Sense comparator offset $V_{\text{ACL(offset)}}$: $\pm 3\text{ mV}$ max.
  * `Q3` (`BSC034N06NS`) $R_{\text{DS(on)}}$ at $V_{\text{GS}} = 7.5\text{ V}$ (`LM5146` $V_{\text{CC}} = 7.5\text{ V}$): Datasheet specifies `3.4 mΩ` max (`2.8 mΩ` typ) at $V_{\text{GS}} = 10\text{ V}$ and `4.5 mΩ` max (`3.6 mΩ` typ) at $V_{\text{GS}} = 6\text{ V}$. Interpolating at $V_{\text{GS}} = 7.5\text{ V}$ gives $R_{\text{DS(on)}}(25^\circ\text{C}) \approx 3.1\text{ m\Omega}$ typ, `2.6 mΩ` min, `3.9 mΩ` max.
* **Inductor ripple current $\Delta I_L$ (`L1 = 10 µH`, $V_{\text{OUT}} = 24\text{ V}$, $f_{\text{SW}} \approx 300\text{ kHz}$ with `R12 = 12 kΩ` or `400 kHz`):**
  * At low battery ($V_{\text{IN}} = 25.5\text{ V}$): $D = 24/25.5 = 0.941$, $\Delta I_L = \frac{(25.5 - 24)\cdot 0.941}{10\text{ \mu H} \cdot f_{\text{SW}}} \approx 0.35\text{–}0.47\text{ A}$.
  * At full 8S battery ($V_{\text{IN}} = 33.6\text{ V}$): $D = 24/33.6 = 0.714$, $\Delta I_L = \frac{(33.6 - 24)\cdot 0.714}{10\text{ \mu H} \cdot f_{\text{SW}}} \approx 1.7\text{–}2.3\text{ A}$.
* **Three Corners:**
  1. **Minimum limit corner (low battery, worst-case negative offset, high $R_{\text{DS(on)}}$):**
     * $V_{\text{sense,min}} = 180\text{ \mu A} \times 217.8\ \Omega - 3\text{ mV} = 39.2\text{ mV} - 3.0\text{ mV} = 36.2\text{ mV}$.
     * With $R_{\text{DS(on),max}} = 3.9\text{ m\Omega}$: $I_{\text{valley,min}} = \frac{36.2\text{ mV}}{3.9\text{ m\Omega}} = 9.28\text{ A}$.
     * Even if `Q3` heats up faster than `U2` ($\Delta T = +25^\circ\text{C}$ uncompensated, $R_{\text{DS(on)}} \approx 4.3\text{ m\Omega}$): $I_{\text{valley,min}} = \frac{36.2\text{ mV}}{4.3\text{ m\Omega}} = 8.42\text{ A}$, giving $I_{\text{DC,min}} = I_{\text{valley,min}} + \frac{\Delta I_L}{2} \approx \mathbf{8.6\text{ A}} > 8.0\text{ A}$. **Passes the 8 A requirement across all corners.**
  2. **Typical corner ($25^\circ\text{C}$, $V_{\text{IN}} = 29.6\text{ V}$):**
     * $V_{\text{sense,typ}} = 200\text{ \mu A} \times 220\ \Omega = 44.0\text{ mV}$; $I_{\text{valley,typ}} = \frac{44.0\text{ mV}}{3.1\text{ m\Omega}} = \mathbf{14.2\text{ A}}$ ($I_{\text{DC,typ}} \approx 15.0\text{ A}$, $I_{\text{peak,typ}} \approx 15.8\text{ A}$).
  3. **Maximum limit / inductor saturation corner (33.6 V battery, positive offset, minimum $R_{\text{DS(on)}} = 2.6\text{ m\Omega}$):**
     * $V_{\text{sense,max}} = 220\text{ \mu A} \times 222.2\ \Omega + 3\text{ mV} = 51.9\text{ mV}$.
     * $I_{\text{valley,max}} = \frac{51.9\text{ mV}}{2.6\text{ m\Omega}} = 19.96\text{ A}$.
     * With $\Delta I_L \approx 2.3\text{ A}$ (plus ~10–15% inductance roll-off near 22 A): $I_{\text{peak,max}} \approx 19.96 + 1.3 = \mathbf{21.3\text{ A}}$ (or up to ~`24 A` during a hard short-circuit before comparator propagation delay).
     * Against the reverted **Coilcraft `XAL1510-103MED`** ($I_{\text{sat}} = 26.3\text{ A}$ for 30% drop at $25^\circ\text{C}$, $I_{\text{rms}} = 22\text{ A}$, 60 V rated), **21.3–24 A peak has a solid margin below saturation**. `R13 = 220 Ω` with `XAL1510-103MED` is well-centered.
