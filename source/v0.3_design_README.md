# Robot PDB v0.3: main board with one USB for audio and servos

This is the v0.2 main board plus a new right-hand section. The board is now 228 x 108 mm (it was 177 x 108). It is still 4 layers.

The new section is driven by **one USB-C cable from the Jetson**. On the board, a hub chip splits that cable three ways:

| What | Chip | What the Jetson sees |
|---|---|---|
| USB hub | WCH CH334F (4 ports) | a USB hub |
| Stereo speaker amp | C-Media CM108B USB audio chip, feeding a TI TPA3118D2 class-D amp (2 x ~15 W into 4 ohm at 12V) | a USB sound card. Speaker wires go into two screw terminals. |
| Both Dynamixel buses | WCH CH342F dual serial chip, with two SN74LVC2G241 half-duplex buffers (works like a U2D2) | two serial ports: XL330 bus (5V) and XL430 bus (12V) |
| Spare | 2 hub ports on 4-pin header pads (not fitted) | spare USB ports |

The power section is unchanged from v0.2: battery input, motor pass-through, 24V Thor rail, 3x 5V, 2x 11.7V. The amp runs from the 11.7V audio rail. That rail's XT30 is still fitted as a "12V AUX" output.

## Status
- Routed. KiCad DRC: **0 unconnected pads, 0 clearance or short errors.** The remaining DRC items are silkscreen overlaps and "library not found" warnings, the same cosmetic set as v0.2.
- The inner layer 1 ground plane is kept solid. This version forbids autorouted signals on it.
- Schematic and PCB netlists cross-checked: 0 mismatches.

## Rough cost
About **$320-370 for 5 bare boards with 2 assembled**. That is up from about $250-280 for v0.2. The increase comes from:
- the bigger board
- about 9 new extended parts at about $3 each
- about $12 of extra parts per board

As before, the real number comes from uploading `main-board/jlcpcb/` at jlcpcb.com.

## Still to do before ordering (adds to the v0.2 list)
1. **Confirm the new parts' LCSC numbers.** I checked these on LCSC: CH334F C5187527, CH342F C2841530, CM108B C371347 (only ~700 in stock), TPA3118D2DAPR C46497. The USB-C jack, ESD chip, crystal, ferrites, screw terminals and buffers are filled from memory and marked "confirm" in `docs/BOM_annotated.csv`. These still need an LCSC number: the 680nF speaker filter caps, the 5.6k gain resistor and the 10uH amp inductors. The amp inductors are the same Sunlord part as the 12V rails.
2. **USB layout.** The Jetson-to-hub link is USB 2.0 high speed. Its traces are short (under 15 mm), but they are not impedance-controlled pairs. A reviewer should look at the USB-C to ESD to hub path.
3. **Amp settings.** Gain is 20 dB, the AM-avoidance switching frequency is 400 kHz and power limit is off. Mute is held off by a resistor.
4. **Servo data lines.** They have 10k pull-ups to the USB 5V. The buffers run from USB 5V, so with USB unplugged the servos keep power but get no data.
5. The optional external-U2D2 headers (J309/J409) are not fitted. You can solder them on if you want to bypass the on-board interface.

## Regenerate
`generator/` holds the scripts:
- `bigboard.py` places the parts and runs Freerouting (about 40 minutes for the main pass).
- `postfix.py` hand-finishes the last few connections and adds the ground stitching vias, then refills and runs DRC.
- `make_outputs.py` writes the JLC files.

The mini and test boards are unchanged: use `../v0.2/mini-board` and `../v0.2/test-board`.
